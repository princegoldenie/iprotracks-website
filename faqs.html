Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to the most common questions from students and learners.

Is iProTracks free?

Some introductory courses are free. Advanced tracks may require enrollment or activation depending on the program.

Can I really earn while learning?

Yes. The platform is designed to help you build skills that connect directly to earning opportunities, internships, and digital work.

Do I get a certificate?

Yes. Certificates are awarded after successful completion of course requirements and assessments.

Who can join IPPN?

Anyone globally can join IPPN iProTracks regardless of background or experience level.
